Music Ministry

Worship Through Song & Sound

A Place for Every Voice

Music is central to worship at Brookings First UMC. From handbell choirs to the Chancel Choir to our contemporary Worship Team, there are opportunities for musicians of all ages and abilities to use their gifts in service to God and the congregation.

Choirs & Handbells

Chiming Children

Wednesdays · 4:00 PM · Grades 3–5

Younger students learn to ring handbells in a fun, structured setting. The Chiming Children group meets Wednesday afternoons and participates in worship throughout the year.

Director: Lynette Smith

Youth Bells

Wednesdays · 5:00–5:45 PM · Grades 6–12

Middle and high school students come together for handbell rehearsal Wednesday evenings. Youth Bells is a wonderful way for young people to grow musically and serve the congregation through worship.

Jubilee Ringers

Tuesdays · 7:00 PM

Our adult handbell ensemble rehearses Tuesday evenings and performs in worship throughout the year. All skill levels welcome — come ring with us!

Director: Brenda Hieb
605-691-9395

Chancel Choir

Wednesdays · 7:00 PM

Our adult choir sings in worship services and special events throughout the year. Childcare is available during rehearsals, making it easy for parents to participate.

All voice parts welcome — come as you are and add your voice to ours.

Contemporary Worship & Media

Worship Team

Contemporary Worship

Our Worship Team leads contemporary music in worship services. If you play an instrument, sing, or run sound — we'd love to hear from you. Fill out the interest form to connect with the team.

Media Ministry

Audio, Video & Livestream

Our media team handles sound, video production, and livestreaming of worship services. This ministry ensures that worshippers both in the sanctuary and online can fully participate.

Interested in joining the media team? Reach out to Sam Becker.
