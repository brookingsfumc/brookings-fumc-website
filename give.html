Giving FAQ

Yes. All online giving is processed through Vanco, a trusted payment provider used by thousands of churches and nonprofits across the country. Vanco uses bank-level SSL encryption and is PCI DSS compliant — the same security standard used by major banks and credit card companies. Brookings First UMC never sees or stores your payment information.

Yes. Brookings First United Methodist Church is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. All charitable contributions are tax-deductible to the extent permitted by law. You will receive a giving statement from the church each January for gifts made during the prior calendar year. For online gifts, you will also receive an immediate email receipt from Vanco.

Setting up recurring giving is easy through our Vanco portal. When you click "Give Now," you'll see an option to make your gift recurring — choose your frequency (weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly) and your start date. You can also set up recurring giving through the Vanco Faith mobile app. Recurring gifts help our church plan and budget throughout the year, and we are deeply grateful for that steady support.

You can update or cancel your recurring gift at any time by logging into your Vanco account at secure.myvanco.com, or through the Vanco Faith mobile app. If you need help, contact our church office at office@brookingsmethodist.org or call us at 605-692-4345 and we'll be happy to assist you.

ACH (Automated Clearing House) giving pulls funds directly from your checking or savings account — similar to a direct debit. The main advantage is that ACH gifts carry no processing fees, meaning 100% of your gift goes directly to the church. Credit and debit card gifts are also very welcome, though a small processing fee is applied by Vanco. You can choose your payment method when giving through the Vanco portal.

Yes! When giving through the Vanco portal, you can designate your gift to a specific fund — such as the general fund, building fund, or a specific mission or outreach project. If you don't see the fund you're looking for, contact the church office and we can help direct your gift appropriately.
