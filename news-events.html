Volunteer

HyVee Receipt Tabulator Needed

Thank you to everyone who faithfully submits HyVee receipts! Through this program, we receive around $2,000 each year to support educational ministries. A special thank you goes to Peg Page, who has faithfully tabulated the receipts for several years — we are so grateful for her dedication!

Peg would like to pass this responsibility on to a new volunteer. The tabulating can be done on your own schedule at home, even while watching TV or relaxing in the evening. If you're interested, please contact Peg at 605-692-4345 or finance@brookingsmethodist.org, or reach out to Gretchen at education@brookingsmethodist.org.

Outreach

#onething — May: Habitat for Humanity

During May, in recognition of Father's Day, for just $10 you can honor a loved one — your father, uncle, grandfather, or friend — and donate to the local Habitat for Humanity chapter. Forms are available at the Welcome Center and in the office.

Worship

Radio Broadcast Sponsorships

There are still slots available to sponsor a Radio Broadcast! Upcoming available dates are June 7, June 21, and June 28. The cost is $60 per date, airing on KBRK 1430 AM every Sunday morning at 9:30 AM. To sponsor a broadcast, contact the church office at 605-692-4345. Pastoral Care

Your Church Cares!

Your church friends care about you! We would like to support you through life's ups and downs — illnesses, surgeries, new babies, loss in the family, marriage, divorce, or any other life event. We have a prayer chain and visitation ministry ready to help. But if we don't know, we can't help. Please give the church office a call at 605-692-4345 and let us know what is happening in your life. We want to put our Love into Action.
