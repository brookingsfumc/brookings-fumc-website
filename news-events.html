Programming

Fall Programming Schedule

We're excited to launch our fall programming schedule at Brookings First UMC. Here's what's happening each week:

Sunday Mornings
9:00 AM — Sunday School (all ages)
9:30 AM — Radio Broadcast on KBRK 1430 AM
10:00 AM — Sunday Worship

Wednesday Evenings
5:00 PM — Manna in the Middle (Community Meal)
6:00 PM — Manna in the Middle (Worship)
7:00 PM — Youth Group, Wednesday School & Chancel Choir Rehearsal

All are welcome — we hope to see you there!

Worship

August 23 — Church Service at Pioneer Park

Join us on August 23rd for our Fall Kickoff in the Park as we welcome our college students back for the new school year! We'd love to enjoy a delicious brunch together before and after the service. If you are willing to bring a brunch item or treat to share, please let Maria know at worship@brookingsmethodist.org, or simply bring it with you to the park that morning.

Employment

Campus Ministry Director — Now Hiring

FUMC is looking to hire a Campus Ministry Director. A summary of the position can be found on the Welcome Center desk. If you know of someone who may be interested, please direct them to our website at brookingsmethodist.org or contact Pastor Jen.

Worship

Radio Broadcast Sponsorships

Only 4 slots remain to be filled for the year. Dates still open are: October 18, November 15, November 29, and December 13. Your donation of $60 per broadcast helps fund this important ministry. The weekly broadcast airs on KBRK 1430 AM every Sunday morning at 9:30 AM. To sponsor a broadcast, contact the church office at 605-692-4345. Please make checks payable to FUMC and note "Radio Broadcast" on the memo line.

Outreach

#onething — August: Brookings Backpack

During August, the Mission Committee's focus for #onething is Brookings Backpack. Donations of single serving applesauce or fruit cups would be welcomed. Drop them off at the church office or Welcome Center.

Pastor's Office Hours

Public Office Hours — August 27

There will not be Public Office Hours this week. Stop by on Wednesday, August 27th and visit with Pastor Jen at Kool Beans Coffee and Roasterie from 1:00–3:00 PM. No agenda needed — just come as you are!

Milestone

Happy 90th Birthday, Jean Pierce!

Please join us in celebrating Jean Pierce, who turned 90 years old on August 17th. What a wonderful milestone — congratulations, Jean!

Volunteer

Sunday Bus Drivers Needed

We could use another driver (or two!) for the Sunday bus. If you would like to find out more information, please reach out to Charlie Schnabel at 605-695-9806.

Pastoral Care

Your Church Cares!

Your church friends care about you! We would like to support you through life's ups and downs — illnesses, surgeries, new babies, loss in the family, marriage, divorce, or any other life event. We have a prayer chain and visitation ministry ready to help. But if we don't know, we can't help. Please give the church office a call at 605-692-4345 and let us know what is happening in your life. We want to put our Love into Action.
