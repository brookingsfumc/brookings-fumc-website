Pastor's Office Hours

Public Office Hours with Pastor Jen

Join Pastor Jen for informal Public Office Hours at Kool Beans Coffee and Roasterie. Stop by, grab a cup of coffee, and have a conversation — no agenda needed. Check the bulletin or contact the church office for upcoming dates and times.

Facilities

Spring Clean Up

Help us spruce up our church home! We'll be tackling outdoor tidying and a deep cleaning of the Community Life Center kitchen. Many hands make light work — all are welcome to pitch in. Contact the church office for the current scheduled date and time.

Outreach

#onething — Blanket Drive

This month's #onething initiative is a blanket drive. Donate a blanket ($10 each) in honor or remembrance of a mother, aunt, grandmother, or friend who has made a difference in your life. Donation forms are available at the Welcome Center and the church office.

Pastoral Care

Your Church Cares!

Did you know Brookings First UMC has an active prayer chain and visitation ministry? If you or someone you know is experiencing a life event — illness, loss, a new baby, or anything else — please give the church office a call at 605-692-4345. We want to show up for you.

Giving

Give Online with the Vanco App

Giving to Brookings First UMC is easy with the Vanco Payment Solutions mobile app, available for Apple and Android devices. Simply search "Brookings" and select First United Methodist Church. You can also give online any time at our giving page. Questions? Call us at 605-692-4345.
